# Original (`fa25dca`) vs. `v9.1` Track Thickness Comparison

The two screenshots show the original baseline PCB (`fa25dca:bldc_motor_cntroller/bldc_motor_cntroller.kicad_pcb`, zoomed on `U2` pins `29–56`, the `C24..C22` `0402` passive row, `GVDD`, and the M1 MOSFET row). That original board was routed on a `1 oz` rule set (`Default = 0.12 mm` track / `0.12 mm` clearance, `HV = 0.30 mm` track / `0.12 mm` clearance) with manual multi-width overrides between fine-pitch `DRV8301` pin fanouts and post-passive power/gate trunks.

## 1. Exact Measured Track Widths by Net Group

| Signal / Net Group | Visible in Screenshots (`U2` / M1 Region) | Original `fa25dca` Widths (mm) | Current `v9.1` Netclass Width (mm) | Key Difference & Routing Impact |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Post-resistor MOSFET Gates** (`R_gate.2 -> Q1..Q12` Gate) | Heavy vertical/angled `F.Cu` traces exiting bottom of `R34, R33, R29, R28, R25, R24` into FET gates | **`0.50`** (`F.Cu`) | `0.30` (`Gate`) | `v9.1` made FET gate traces **narrower** (`0.30 mm` vs `0.50 mm`). On `DirectFET_L8` (`-90°`), Gate Pad `2` is `1.30 × 0.70 mm` inside a `2.55 mm` channel (`>= 0.60 mm` margin at `0.50 mm` width) and `0402` `R_gate` pad width is `0.64 mm`, so **`0.50 mm`** fits cleanly with zero vias. |
| **Pre-resistor Driver Gate Outputs** (`U1/U2` `GH_A..GL_C -> R_gate.1`) | Thin `F.Cu` fanout stubs from `U2` pins `35, 37, 39, 41, 45, 47` to top of `R34..R24` | **`0.20`** (`0.12` on `M1 GH_A/GL_A`) | `0.30` (`Gate`) | Grouping `GH_*/GL_*` with `Q*-G` at `0.30 mm` made traces nearly as wide as `U1/U2`'s `0.3048 mm` pads (`0.50 mm` pitch), blocking diagonal `F.Cu` escape and forcing `B.Cu` vias. **`0.20 mm`** matches the original fanout. |
| **Bootstrap Caps** (`U1/U2` `BST_A/B/C -> C_bst.2`) | Thin `F.Cu` stubs from `U2` pins `38, 42, 48` to `C34.2, C33.2, C32.2` | **`0.20`** (`0.12` on `C32`) | `0.30` | **`0.20 mm`** matches the original `F.Cu` pin-to-cap stub and avoids `U1/U2` pad-entry crowding. |
| **Driver Phase / Shunt Sense & Bootstrap Return** (`U1/U2` `SH_A..C`, `SL_B..C`) | `0.12–0.20 mm` from `U2` pins `34, 36, 40, 46` to `C34.1/C33.1/C32.1`, then **`0.50 mm`** south to FET phase pours (`0.80–1.00 mm` on `B.Cu` `SL_B/SL_C` in original) | **`0.12–0.20`** at `U1/U2` pins; **`0.50`** (`SH_*`) / **`0.80–1.00`** (`SL_*`) to pours | `0.30` uniform (`Power`, plus `0.25 mm` DRU clearance) | `v9.1` carries bulk `SH_*`/`SL_*` current in `6.5–8.5 mm` `F.Cu+B.Cu` pours, so the `U1/U2` tap only carries bootstrap/sense current; using **`0.20 mm`** at `U1/U2` (or `0.20 mm` pin tap + `0.40–0.50 mm` pour tie) eliminates `U1/U2` congestion. |
| **Kelvin Current-Sense Inputs & Outputs** (`SP1/SN1`, `SP2/SN2`, `NT1..8`, `SO1/SO2`) | Very thin coupled traces exiting `U2` pins `30–33` into `C24, C23` and south to shunts | **`0.12`** (`F.Cu` / `B.Cu`) | `0.20` (`Sense`) | Original `0.12 mm` was for `1 oz` copper and **violates** the `0.15 mm` (`6 mil`) minimum on `v9.1`'s `70 µm` (`2 oz`) outer copper. **`0.16 mm`** (with `0.16–0.18 mm` gap/clearance) is the closest `2 oz`-safe equivalent (`+10 µm` margin over `0.15 mm` fab min) and opens room around `C2/C3` and `C23/C24`. |
| **`GVDD`, `+5V`, `+3.3V`, `+3.3VA` Supplies** | Wide **`0.50 mm`** diagonal `GVDD` trunk on right side of `C22`, stepping down to **`0.20 mm`** into `C22.2` / `U2.49` (`+3V3`) / `U2.29` (`VDC`) | **`0.50`** trunks (`0.80–1.00` local `+5V`), **`0.12–0.20`** IC pin entries | `0.30` (`Supply`), `0.50` (`Supply_5V`) | Raise `GVDD` / `+3.3VA` trunks to **`0.40–0.50 mm`** where clear, while keeping **`0.20 mm`** pin-entry stubs into `0.50 mm`-pitch `U1/U2/U6` pads. |
| **Digital Signals** (`PWM`, `SPI`, `ENC`, `GPIO`, `CAN_D/R`, `TEMP`, `nFAULT`) | Thin upper-board signal buses into `U6` (`LQFP-64`, `0.50 mm` pitch) and `U2` | **`0.12`** & **`0.20`** | `0.20` (`Default`) | On `2 oz` (`70 µm`) outer copper, `0.12 mm` is below the `0.15 mm` fab limit; **`0.16–0.18 mm`** (or keeping `0.20 mm` where uncongested) preserves `2 oz` manufacturability while easing `LQFP-64` / `HTSSOP-56` fanout. |
| **USB Differential Pair** (`USB_DP`, `USB_DN`) | Near `J4` / `R52` / `R53` (`TP5` top-left) | `0.261` (`F.Cu`), `0.191` (`In1.Cu`) | `0.155` / `0.220` gap (`USB`) | `v9.1` uses `70 µm` `F.Cu` `0.11 mm` over solid `In1.Cu` `GND`, requiring **`0.155 mm` width / `0.220 mm` gap** (`90.2–90.65 Ω` field-solved). |

## 2. Recommended `2 oz`-Safe Netclass Split for `v9.1`

1. **Split `Gate` into two netclasses**:
   - `Gate_FET` (`Net-(Q1-G)` .. `Net-(Q12-G)`): **`0.50 mm`** track width, `0.18 mm` clearance (`0` vias, `F.Cu` lateral exit from `DirectFET_L8` Pad 2 straight to `R_gate.2`).
   - `Gate_Drv` (`GH_A..GL_C`, `Net-(U1/U2-BST_A..C)`, `Net-(Q13/Q14-G)`, `Net-(U3-HO/LO)`): **`0.20 mm`** track width, `0.18 mm` clearance (clean single-layer `F.Cu` fanout from `0.50 mm`-pitch `U1/U2` pins to `0402` passives).
2. **Separate `DRV8301` Phase/Bus Taps from Heavy Pour Rules**:
   - Keep `VDC`, `SH_A..C`, `SL_B..C`, `HS` bulk current in the `6.5–8.5 mm` `F.Cu+B.Cu` pours, and set routed driver sense/bootstrap tap width to **`0.20 mm`** entering `U1/U2` (`0.40–0.50 mm` from `C_bst` to phase pours where clear).
3. **Tighten `Sense` & Fine-Pitch Signal Widths within `2 oz` (`70 µm`) Fab Limits**:
   - Set `Sense` (`SP1/SN1`, `SP2/SN2`, `NT*`, `SO1/SO2`) to **`0.16 mm`** (`>= 0.15 mm` `2 oz` fab floor, `0.16 mm` clearance / diff gap) and `Default` digital signals to **`0.16–0.18 mm`** so `U1`, `U2`, and `U6` (`0.50 mm` pitch) fan out cleanly like the original board without violating `2 oz` copper rules.
