# Placement Review of Unrouted Components (`bldc_motor_cntroller_v9_11.kicad_pcb`)

- **Target board (read-only audit, no files modified):** `bldc_motor_cntroller_v9_10/bldc_motor_cntroller_v9_11.kicad_pcb` (`329` footprints, `1,329` segments, `605` vias, `111` zones).
- **Current DRC state (`.copperpilot/tmp/review-drc.json`):** `217` `unconnected_items` across `122` unique footprints, plus `6` DRC errors (`2` pre-existing clearance errors at `U7.5`/`J5.A6` and `4` new errors on `R54` overlapping the `F.Cu` `VDC` pour).
- **Breakdown of the `122` unrouted footprints:**
  - **`45` footprints** have signal, supply, or power-stage disconnects (`99` of `217` DRC items).
  - **`77` footprints** are missing **only** `GND` and/or `+3.3V` plane ties (`118` of `217` DRC items — **54%** of all remaining disconnects) and **do not need to be moved**.

## 2. Unrouted Components That Should **NOT** Be Moved

| Group | Components | Count | Why Keeping Them Fixed Is Better |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Edge & Power Connectors** | `J5` (USB-C), `J6–J12` (JST-SH), `J1–J4` | 11 | Mechanically fixed to board edges/harness. `J5.A6` (`USB_J4_DP`) is a `0.50 mm`-pitch pad-entry issue, not a placement issue. |
| **Power MOSFETs & Bulk Caps** | `Q3–Q14` (`DirectFET_L8`), `C49–C52` (`100 µF` bulk) | 16 | Mechanically locked by the `±5 mm` heatsink bar (`H1`/`H4` at `y = 96.0 mm`) and user-approved bulk-cap positions. `Q4`/`Q10` only need their south `GND` source pads tied to the adjacent `F.Cu` `GND` pour. |
| **MCU (`U9`)** | `U9` (`STM32F405RGTx`) | 1 | `51` signal nets (including matched `USB_DP/DN`, `Y1` crystal, `SPI3`, and `12×` PWM) are already routed to `U9`. Moving the `0402` bypass caps `0.8–1.0 mm` outward fixes the perimeter pinch at a fraction of the rework. |
| **LDOs (`U3`, `U4`) & OVP (`R1`, `U6`)** | `U3`, `U4`, `R1`, `U6` | 4 | `U3.1`/`U3.3` (`+5V`) are `1.8 mm` from an existing `+5V` track end; `R1.1` (`VDC`) just needs one trace south to the `VDC` plane. Moving them disturbs already-routed OVP/`+3.3VA` tracks for little gain. |
| **Far-West Thermistor (`M1` Temp 2)** | `C113`, `R61`, `TH4` (`4.4..8.0, 84.5`) | 3 | Isolated 3-pad RC network (`TEMP_SENSE_2`); already side-by-side at `1.8 mm` pitch — needs only two `1.8 mm` `F.Cu` segments. |
| **Plane-Tie-Only Components (`GND` / `+3.3V`)** | `77` footprints (`12×` HS-drain HF caps `C79–C134`, `C151–C158`, `D8`, `U10`, `U11`, `C22–C29`, `C58–C59`, `C104–C105`, `NT2`, `NT6`, etc.) | 77 | Account for **`118 / 217` (`54%`)** of all DRC unconnected items. Most already have a dedicated `GND` or `+3.3V` via `0.7–1.2 mm` from the pad and only need short `F.Cu` stub tracks or local via drops — moving them adds zero benefit. |
